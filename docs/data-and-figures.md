## What counts as reproduction

The [numeric comparison policy](figure-manifest.json) gates the synthetic
scene fingerprint and candidate geometry rather than requiring identical font
rasterization across machines. A matching screenshot is weaker evidence than
the registered numeric assertions.

The [results runbook](../results/README.md) describes how a future verified
Earth Engine export should be composed and attributed. The existing legacy
rendering is deliberately not used as the README's main evidence.

No new imagery, prediction, synthetic result or holdout judgment was generated
by the September 10 repository presentation update.
